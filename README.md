**Sky Courier — Island Run** is an original browser flight game: fly a little red mail plane in chase view over a procedural island — bay, river valley, farmland, harbour town and a northern ridge — through golden sky rings and back down onto the runway. Three sorties (Ring Run, Courier Delivery, Landing Challenge) with fuel to mind, damage on rough touchdowns, a live HUD (airspeed, altitude, compass tape, artificial horizon, throttle and fuel), and fully synthesised engine, wind and chime sounds. No libraries, no network: plain static files under `game/`, drawn on canvas.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Fly with W/S to pitch, A/D to bank, Shift/Ctrl for throttle, Space for the airbrake — or hold the mouse and steer toward the pointer. Press 1–3 to pick a mission, Enter to fly.
